How to Read Your Explanation of Benefits (EOB)

1 Billed Amount

The provider's gross chargemaster rate. In-network patients NEVER pay this full rate.

2 Allowed Amount

The maximum rate your insurer and provider agreed upon contractually.

3 Plan Paid

The portion your health plan paid directly to the clinic on your behalf.

4 Patient Responsibility

Your true legal out-of-pocket obligation (deductible, copay, or coinsurance).
